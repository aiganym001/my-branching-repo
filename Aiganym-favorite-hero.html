Who is Spider-Man?

Spider-Man is one of the most famous superheroes in the Marvel universe. His real name is Peter Parker. He is a young man who received amazing abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

Peter Parker lives in New York City and uses his powers to protect people and fight crime.

Spider-Man's Superpowers

Spider Sense

He can sense danger before it happens.

Super Strength

He has incredible strength and agility.

Web Shooting

He uses webs to swing between buildings and catch criminals.

Wall Climbing

He can climb walls and ceilings like a spider.

His Story

Peter Parker was an ordinary student until a radioactive spider changed his life. After gaining superpowers, he learned that great power comes with great responsibility.

Spider-Man teaches us to be brave, kind, responsible and always help others.

Why I Like Spider-Man

I like Spider-Man because he is brave, funny and helpful. Even though he has many problems in his personal life, he never gives up and always protects others.

"With great power comes great responsibility."
