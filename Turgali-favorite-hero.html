Who Is Hulk?

Hulk is one of the most famous superheroes in the Marvel Comics universe. His real name is Dr. Bruce Banner. He is a brilliant scientist who was accidentally exposed to a massive amount of gamma radiation during an experiment. After this accident, Bruce gained the ability to transform into a giant, incredibly strong green superhero known as Hulk. Hulk is famous for his enormous strength, green skin and powerful personality. Although he sometimes struggles to control his anger, he often uses his abilities to protect innocent people and save the world.

Hulk's Origin Story

Bruce Banner was a talented scientist who worked with gamma radiation.

During a dangerous experiment, he was exposed to a huge amount of gamma energy while saving another person.

This accident changed his life forever. Whenever Bruce experiences intense emotions, especially anger, he transforms into the Incredible Hulk.

Over time, Bruce learns more about himself and tries to control the powerful creature inside him. His story is about accepting yourself, controlling your emotions and using your strength for good.
